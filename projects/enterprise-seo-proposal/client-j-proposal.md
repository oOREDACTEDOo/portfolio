## The 20-Slide Structure (with intent)

### **SECTION 1: Framing & Confidence (Slides 1–4)**

### **1. Title / Why Us**

**Purpose:** Set tone, not sell features

- SEO + Content for Enterprise Scale
- Migration-safe, brand-led, AI-ready

> Signal: You’ve done this before.
> 

---

### **2. Our Understanding of Client J / Case Studies**

**Purpose:** Show deep empathy

- Multi-brand consolidation
- Localised demand
- High-intent, time-sensitive searches
- Complex internal expertise

> This is where evaluators lean in.
> 

---

### **3. The Core Growth Problem**

**Purpose:** Align on the real challenge

Frame it as tension:

- National authority **vs** local intent
- Education **vs** conversion
- Legacy content **vs** future AI visibility

---

### **4. What Success Looks Like (12–24 Months)**

**Purpose:** Anchor expectations

- Organic as a demand engine, not traffic
- Reduced reliance on paid for core terms
- Search + AI visibility reinforcing brand leadership

---

### **SECTION 2: Opportunity & Strategy (Slides 5–9)**

### **5. Search Opportunity Landscape**

**Purpose:** Commercial focus

- High-value equipment categories
- Local modifiers (hire near me, city, job-type)
- Research → rental intent journey

*This is where light keyword research earns trust.*

---

### **6. Personas & Search Behaviour**

**Purpose:** Prove customer-led thinking

Map:

- Trades
- Construction managers
- Procurement
- Emergency / infrastructure

Tie each persona to:

- Content type
- Local need
- Decision speed

---

### **7. Pillar-Led Content Strategy (12 Months)**

**Purpose:** Show a scalable system

- Core category pillars
- Supporting guides, FAQs, use-cases
- Conversion pathways

> This is where you out-enterprise competitors.
> 

---

### **8. Local SEO at Scale**

**Purpose:** Show you understand operational reality

- How you'll handle inventory visibility without creating thin content
- Location authority beyond NAP citations
- How local content ties to conversion (not just rankings)

> Emphasise quality at scale, not automation.
> 

---

### **9. Brand Consolidation & Migration Strategy**

**Purpose:** De-risk the biggest fear

- Pre & post migration intelligence
- URL + entity preservation
- Branded search

This slide alone builds confidence.

---

### **SECTION 3: Authority & AEO (Slides 10–13)**

### **10. Turning Internal Experts into Search Assets**

**Purpose:** Leverage what competitors can’t copy

- SMEs → on-site authority
- SMEs → off-site digital PR
- Thought leadership, not blog churn

---

### **11. Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)**

**Purpose:** Show future-readiness

- How LLMs source answers
- Structuring content for retrieval
- Owning factual brand narratives

> This is where you differentiate from “SEO agencies”.
> 

---

### **12. On-Site + Off-Site Authority Model**

**Purpose:** Connect dots

- Content ↔ links ↔ brand signals
- Editorial, not link farming
- Industry relevance over volume

---

### **13. Competitive Advantage Over Time**

**Purpose:** Long-term moat

- Hard-to-replicate content
- Entity strength
- Compounding authority

---

### **SECTION 4: Execution & Governance (Slides 14–17)**

### **14. The Operating Model**

**Purpose:** “How this actually runs”

- Cadence / proactive
- Collaboration with internal teams
- Approval workflows

Enterprise buyers care about this deeply.

---

### **15. The Team**

**Purpose:** Reduce delivery risk

- Strategists
- Technical SEO
- Content leadership
- Clear ownership

> Name roles, not just titles.
> 

---

### **16. Reporting & Measurement**

**Purpose:** Accountability

- Commercial KPIs
- Leading indicators
- AEO visibility tracking
- Local performance views

---

### **17. Prioritisation Framework**

**Purpose:** Show discipline

- Impact vs effort
- Revenue weighting
- What we say *no* to

---

### **SECTION 5: Commitment & Close (Slides 18–20)**

### **18. First 90 Days**

**Purpose:** Momentum

- Discovery
- Technical foundations
- Priority pillars
- Migration safety checks

---

### **19. 12-Month Roadmap Snapshot / Case Studies**

**Purpose:** Long-term confidence

Quarterly view:

- Similar scale/complexity projects
- Equipment rental or adjacent B2B industries
- Measurable outcomes in 6-12 months

---

### **20. Why This Works for Client J**

**Purpose:** Emotional close

- Built for scale
- Built for risk
- Built for leadership
