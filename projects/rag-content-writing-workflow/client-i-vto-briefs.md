## SEO CAREER GUIDES

Use this format for long-form "How to become a [role]" articles targeting specific
search queries. These are different from awareness content — they're research-heavy,
stats-driven, and built around a specific career path.

**On links:**
- Internal links from published_content_map are blog/news pages only — course pages,
  apply pages, and guide downloads are NOT in that table. Add them explicitly in the
  INTERNAL LINKS field of each brief below.
- External links come from Tavily research. The RESEARCH SOURCES field tells Tavily
  what to look for. Claude will cite and link these inline (one link per source,
  first mention only).

Don't fabricate salary figures — let Tavily surface real data.
Don't use vague career language ("endless opportunities", "exciting pathways").
Be specific about roles, titles, and what the work actually involves.

TONE NOTE: Informed, direct, insider knowledge. "Let me show you what this

INTERNAL LINKS (add course and conversion pages here — not in published_content_map):

- Role specifics: [add any industry body or association relevant to this role]
⚠️ REQUIRES: Real alumni story with specific details before submitting.
Add name, roles held, timeline, and any recognition to the INCLUDE section above.
